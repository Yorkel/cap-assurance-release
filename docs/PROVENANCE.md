# Evidence boundaries

The principal comparison uses eight retained GPT-4o configurations on 1,200 sampled questions (1,199 complete cases). The five later repeats have all 6,000 saved raw responses. These records support offline recomputation. The historical experiment had 17 configurations; its aggregate records and recovered item summary are supplied separately and are not a complete historical raw-response archive. Reconstructed vendor labels contain imputation; the principal matched-item results exclude them. A specific historical example is identified as a recorded disagreement, not an imputed output.

The later repeats use an unpinned alias and were made months later. Comparisons are descriptive rather than a contemporaneous causal control. Extracted probability fields are scores from an implemented token-selection rule, not validated final-answer confidence. Routing is hypothetical and no reviewer performance is measured.

The documentary study covers 37 units (19 UK, 18 EU). Official web pages are eligible. The 15 AI Insights chapters count as one unit; some other units have companion files. The sample was frozen on 31 October 2026 against a 26 October selection cut-off. The 2023 Commission staff guidance is the recorded substitute for the inaccessible 2025 version. The Member States' national guidance is outside scope.

Two initial coding passes were AI-assisted. Their entries were reconciled with automated quote matching and author checking of entries, themes and interpretations. This is not independent human double-coding or an inter-coder reliability measurement. Analytic placements in different cells are retained; their counts are not unique passages. The theme groupings and final interpretations are frozen analytical inputs. Rebuilding counts does not repeat or independently validate those judgements.

No manuscript, author correspondence, agent/session logs, credentials or private Git history is part of this artifact.
